Board Foot Math

A board of 1 in by 6 in and 8 ft long counts 4 board feet by the twelfths rule.

Boundary: arithmetic only Board Foot Math applies the named board-foot rule to the numbers you type: thickness in inches times width in inches times length in feet, over twelve. It applies the rule as written, so it has not seen your boards and does not know the species, the moisture, the actual dressed size or the yard's own rounding, and it gives no verdict that a stack is worth the price on the tag. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: one 1x6 by 8 ft, a pile of ten, the cost of 40 board feet at 6.5, and the linear feet 4 board feet makes.

The board truth

A board of 1 in by 6 in and 8 ft long counts 4 board feet by the twelfths rule.

The pile truth

The pile of 10 boards at 4 board feet each counts 40 board feet in all.

The cost and linear truths

The board feet of 40 at 6.5 per board foot cost 260, and the board feet of 4 in stock of 1 in by 6 in come back to 8 linear feet.

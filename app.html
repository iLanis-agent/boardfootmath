Board Foot Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page applies the named board-foot rule to the numbers you type: thickness in inches times width in inches times length in feet, over twelve. Pile totals multiply the per-board figure, and costs multiply board feet by your price. The dimensions, quantities and prices are yours to measure or read off your own lumber and invoice. It applies the rule as written, so it has not seen your boards and does not know the species, the moisture, the actual dressed size or the yard's own rounding, and it gives no verdict that a stack is worth the price on the tag. Every number here is your own entry.

The board truth

One board in board feet: thickness times width times length, over twelve.

The pile truth

A pile of identical boards: the per-board figure times the quantity.

The cost truth

What a board-foot count costs at your price per board foot.

The linear truth

Back the other way: the linear feet a board-foot count makes in a given stock size.
